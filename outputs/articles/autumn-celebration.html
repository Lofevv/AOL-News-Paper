School Life and Events

Autumn Celebration

On 22 October, the Second Building Hall welcomed girls from Years 8 to 11 for an evening together outside the usual school routine.

On the 22nd of October, the Second Building Hall came alive with music, laughter and excitement as girls from Years 8 to 11 gathered for the school’s Autumn Celebration. Held from 5:00 to 7:00 p.m., the evening was about having fun, spending time together and making memories outside the usual school routine.

There was plenty to keep everyone entertained throughout the evening. From Just Dance to roasting marshmallows together, the activities gave everyone a chance to let loose and enjoy themselves. An open buffet was also provided, giving everyone something to enjoy while catching up with friends. The fireworks added extra excitement and made the evening more special.

What made the celebration enjoyable was not just the activities, but the atmosphere. It was a chance for girls from different year groups to come together, meet new people, strengthen friendships and enjoy each other’s company.

By the end of the evening, there were plenty of laughs and memories to take away. The Autumn Celebration was a reminder that school life is about more than lessons and exams; it is also about friendships, experiences and moments that bring everyone together.
