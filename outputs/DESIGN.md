# Academy of Leaders: The Newspaper

## Direction

A student newspaper set with the restraint of a print broadsheet: warm paper, the school crest’s deep navy, and a small red printer’s mark. Fraunces gives headlines an editorial voice; IBM Plex Sans keeps navigation, notices, and long reads direct. The repeated 01 / 02 / 03 folios carry the existing section sequence into the redesigned layout.

## Palette

| Token | Value | Use |

## Type

- Fraunces 500–700 for the wordmark and editorial headlines.
- IBM Plex Sans 400–700 for body copy, navigation, labels, and notices.
- Body copy stays at 16px or larger, with long article lines capped at 68 characters.

## Components

- The supplied school crest in the masthead, kept in its original colours.
- Separate home, notices, school-life, beyond-the-classroom, and article pages.
- Boxed editorial story panels and notice cards, with no nested cards or decorative gradients.
- Entry, scroll reveal, hover, navigation, and reading-progress motion; all motion respects reduced-motion preferences.
- Keyboard skip link, visible focus, semantic landmarks, and responsive navigation.

## Content boundary

This redesign uses the public copy, notices, and crest image from the supplied Google Sites page. Dates and figures are retained only where that page provides them. It adds no testimonials, statistics, or fictional school mark. The Arabic-edition link points to the existing Arabic site.
